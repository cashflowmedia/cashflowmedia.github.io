A · Metallic 3D

Each letter uses a vertical metal gradient (cream → gold → bronze) + offset dark shadow underneath — same depth treatment as the bars themselves.

B · Engraved (carved-in)

Letters look pressed into the bar surface — dark interior with a thin light catch on the bottom edge (sun from above). Reads as if stamped into metal.

C · Brass nameplate

CFM sits on a small dark-bronze plaque under the bars — like a brass plate at the base of the bar graph. Most overtly "logo with a label."

D · Bar-matched

Each letter uses a darker version of its bar's gradient (C=dark green, F=dark green, M=dark bronze) — letters visually "belong" to their bar.
