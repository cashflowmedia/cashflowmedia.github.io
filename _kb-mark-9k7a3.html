The primary mark

Clean, static, no animation. This is the version that goes on print, embroidery, etched metal, swag — anywhere the animated web version isn't appropriate. Same shapes, no effects layered on top.

Single-color variants

A great mark works in one color. Bars use opacity steps (55%, 78%, 100%) to keep their hierarchy without color, and the $ is rendered as a cutout in the bar — appearing in the card's background color so it reads against any solid bar.
