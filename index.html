FAQ

Common questions.

How fast can I get started?

Most clients are set up the same day. After onboarding, you'll have access to your accounts and BM infrastructure within hours.

What happens if my account gets disabled?

We replace it. On Scale and Enterprise plans, replacements are unlimited and turnaround is typically under 2 hours.

What verticals do you support?

eCommerce, lead generation, apps, content, and many higher-risk and policy-sensitive verticals. Reach out and we'll confirm fit.

Do you handle approvals?

Yes — our team helps with approvals guidance and troubleshooting around Meta's policies as part of our support.

Is there a contract?

No long-term contracts. All plans are monthly and you can cancel anytime.
