FAQ

Common questions.

How fast are replacements?

Subscriptions: typically under 2 hours, 24/7. Jade BM2500 customers see auto-replacement in 5–10 minutes via the distributed pool.

How fast can I get onboarded?

Most clients are running spend the same day. After payment, you'll have account access and BM setup within hours.

Which verticals do you allow?

eCommerce, lead gen, apps, content, iGaming, nutra, crypto, and many policy-sensitive verticals. If you're unsure, message us with the offer and we'll confirm fit.

Do I need to warm up the accounts?

For most setups, no — accounts come pre-warmed. For higher-risk verticals or aggressive scaling, our team will guide you through a short warm-up plan.

What's the difference between Jade BM2500 and a regular subscription?

Subscriptions give you a fixed number of accounts with manual replacements when something goes down. Jade BM2500 is a private distributed pool that auto-rotates so spend never pauses — built for operators who can't afford downtime.

Should I pick a one-time bundle or a subscription?

One-time bundles (Profile FB / BM3 Verified / BM10 Instant) are best for testing and short campaigns. Subscriptions (Solo / Scale / Top Agency / Agency Unlimited) are for ongoing operations that need replacements and 24/7 support.

Are there guarantees?

We guarantee unlimited replacements on subscription tiers and the response times listed. We do not guarantee account approval for any specific creative — that's on Meta's policy team.

How do I reach you?

Telegram, WhatsApp, or the floating button on this page. Direct human reply, 24/7.
