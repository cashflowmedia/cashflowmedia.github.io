The Process

Nine moves to live spend.

Every step labeled by who runs it — You, Us, or the System.

  1. 01
    You ~ 1 minute

    Open the conversation

    Telegram or WhatsApp — no forms, no funnels. We typically reply in under 5 minutes during business hours and under 30 minutes overnight. Skip the introductions and tell us your monthly spend, vertical, and what's broken about your current setup.

  2. 02
    Us ~ 5 minutes

    Quick fit check

    Three questions, no call required: monthly volume, vertical sensitivity, current pain points. We confirm your needs map to a real tier and aren't a policy minefield. If we can't help, we tell you straight — and point you to a provider who can.

  3. 03
    You ~ 2 minutes

    Lock in your bundle

    One-time bundles for testing. Monthly subscriptions when you're ready to scale. The Onyx BM2500 flagship if you operate at $50k/day or above. We send the invoice; you pay via your preferred method.

  4. 04
    Us 4–6 hours

    Provision your accounts

    From paid invoice to login credentials in 4–6 hours during business windows, or first thing the next morning if you pay overnight. Your CRM access is provisioned in the same window — you'll get a private dashboard URL and a passcode.

    ⚡
    Heads up: the CRM may show partial data for the first hour while accounts finish syncing. Real spend numbers populate as soon as your campaigns start firing.
  5. 05
    System live

    Your CRM goes live

    Single pane of glass. Spend per account, status per account, replacement history, daily caps — all of it, all the time. Demo below.

  6. 06
    You 5–7 days

    Run the warm-up week

    First week, every account runs safe creative on small daily budgets so Meta sees normal patterns and unlocks higher caps. We give you the exact recipe — sample creatives, budget cadence, audience structure — further down this page.

  7. 07
    System automatic

    Auto-replacement on disable

    The moment any account gets restricted or disabled, our system spawns a replacement from the Onyx BM2500 reserve, binds your campaigns to it, and notifies you in the CRM. Your cash flow doesn't pause — it shifts spend, automatically.

  8. 08
    Us 24/7

    Eyes on your accounts at all hours

    Real humans on rotation. We catch unusual spend velocity, throttles, policy notices, and pixel issues the moment they show up — most before you'd see them yourself. You can ping us in the same Telegram thread you started in step 1.

  9. 09
    You ongoing

    Scale without a ceiling

    Post-warmup, daily limits jump from $50–$100 to thousands per account per day. Your only constraint becomes ROAS. Need more accounts? Add a bundle and they're live the same day.

Step 6 · Warm-up week

The warm-up recipe we run for every new account.

Five days of safe creative + small budgets. By day six, Meta unlocks higher caps and your campaigns can scale without raising flags. Below: 5 sample ads we know perform during this window.

Brand awareness

"How we ship faster than our competitors"

A short BTS-style image of your team or warehouse. Caption: how you operate. Outcome: brand affinity, no aggressive offer.

Soft offer

"Free shipping on every order this week"

Plain product photo, no urgency stacking, no countdown. Light promotional language Meta has seen a million times.

Educational

"3 things most buyers miss when they pick a [product]"

Listicle carousel with neutral copy. Drives qualified clicks without big claims. Engagement signals stack fast.

Testimonial

"Why I switched after 6 months — Sarah, real customer"

UGC-style video or static with a real-sounding quote. Add Sarah's first name and city. No "results not typical" red flags.

Lead magnet

"Get our free [niche] checklist — 1-page PDF"

Simple form ask in exchange for a free resource. Generates pixel data, builds a custom audience, never trips policy.

Why these work

  • No urgency stacking. "Last chance, only 2 left, ends tonight" is a flag pattern. Soft CTAs don't trigger pre-warmup review.
  • Neutral language. Avoid superlatives, "guaranteed," health/income claims, and exclamation marks while accounts are fresh.
  • Visual variety. Mix lifestyle, product, UGC, and educational. Meta's pattern detector likes diversity.
  • Engagement before conversion. The first 5–7 days, optimize for clicks/views. Switch to purchase events from day 6 once caps unlock.
  • Real audiences. No 3-week-old custom audiences from a different account. Build fresh from your pixel data during warmup.
